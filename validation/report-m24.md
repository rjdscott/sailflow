## Honest weaknesses

- **The whole shape layer is invented.** `rig/state.ts`, `shape/flying.ts` and `shape/toOrc.ts` are sign-correct heuristics with calibration knobs (ADR 0006). Nothing published maps turnbuckle turns to shroud tension, tension to forestay sag, or sag to flying shape for any class. Every magnitude in that chain is an assumption; only the signs are tested.
- **This is one certificate, not a class polar.** An ORC polar is issued per measured hull. ADR 0020 measured the spread across 40 Melges 24 certificates at up to 11.4 %, wider than the 3.0 % boat-speed tolerance the gate applies — so a failing row may be the model, or may be a different boat. The polar file's source block names which.
- **Six rig and shape knobs are unfitted.** No tuning guide is committed for m24, so calibration stage 3 (rig + shape) never ran: `rig.EI`, `rig.turnsToN`, `rig.sagK` and the three `shape.*` knobs hold code defaults fitted against another class's rig. Every dock-tune number this class reports is tier C until a guide is sourced.
- **The calibration below is the one from PR #128, and this round deliberately did not replace it (ADR 0025, ADR 0026).** The hydro changes in that round — the keel effective-draft law and the symmetric heel box in the equilibrium solver — are near-neutral for this class: replayed under the new model, the committed calibration gives the same 6/10 with the same four failing rows as before. A refit *was* run and was rejected on its own terms, not on its hold-out score: it landed in a basin where a **fitted** row has no equilibrium at all — TWS 24 at 90°, heel pinned on the 45° ceiling `solve/equilibrium.ts` calls the point "beyond which the hull model is meaningless", reported at 2.83 kt against a printed 9.43 — so its loss is partly noise. (For the record it scored 4/10.) This class heels 30–45° on its printed reaching rows, which is where that ceiling lives, and its gate has moved 8/10 → 6/10 → 4/10 across three rounds on nothing published. It needs a round of its own, starting with why a 945 kg sportboat with a published righting moment heels to the ceiling at 90° in 16 kt.
- **Heel is unfitted and ungated.** This class's polar publishes no heel column, and since ADR 0022 nothing fits heel on any class anyway (`hydro.crewArmMul` holds its code default); no row below has a heel to compare against. The model heel in the tables is an output nothing checked.
