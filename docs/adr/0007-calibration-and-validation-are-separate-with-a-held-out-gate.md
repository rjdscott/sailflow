# 0007. Calibration and validation are separate, with a held-out polar gate

- **Status:** Superseded by [0012](0012-hold-out-split-by-wind-speed-not-by-angle.md) (the fit/hold-out split) and by [0023](0023-vmg-gate-measures-vmg-lost-at-the-polars-angle.md) (the 2° VMG-angle tolerance); the boat-speed tolerances stand
- **Date:** 2026-08-25

## Context

The brief calls for "reproducing the ORC Speed Guide polar at all seven wind
speeds within a stated tolerance" as validation. The ORC hydro side needs
hull offsets and measured stability that are not public for the J/70, so
resistance must be fitted; the rig and shape layers have no evidence base and
must be fitted too. Fitting roughly a dozen parameters to fourteen VMG points
and then reporting a match is not validation. The polar itself was produced by
VPP 2011 1.02 (issued 2012) while the coefficient tables we implement are the
2023 edition, so part of any residual is ORC's own revisions.

## Options considered

**A. Fit all seven TWS, report the residual** (the brief).
- Pros: simplest; small residuals look impressive.
- Cons: circular; cannot detect overfitting; the build gate is theatre.

**B. Fit on a subset, gate on held-out points** (chosen).
- Pros: an honest out-of-sample number; a real regression gate.
- Cons: fewer points to fit; tolerances must be wide enough to survive the
  2011-vs-2023 model gap.

**C. No fit; use published DSYHS and ORC as-is.**
- Pros: purest.
- Cons: the J/70 sits at the edge of the Delft series; the model would be
  wrong by a lot and the disagreement panel useless.

## Decision

**We will fit hydro and rig/shape free parameters on TWS 6, 10, 12, 16 and 20
(VMG points), freeze them into `data/boats/j70.json`, and validate on the
held-out TWS 8 and 14 VMG points plus the 60°, 90° and 120° rows at every
TWS, because a held-out residual is the only number that says anything about
the model rather than the optimiser.** Stage-4 rig calibration targets the
North base settings at the 8–10 and 12–16 kt bands only; other bands and the
Quantum guide are held out so the disagreement panel is not tautological.

Tolerances (the build gate): held-out VMG boat speed within 3 % and VMG
angle within 2°; held-out 60/90/120 rows within 5 % boat speed (tier B).
Rationale: parametric VPPs of the same lineage typically scatter 2–4 % on
boat speed between editions; 3 % on the points the fit never saw is tight
enough to catch a broken model and loose enough not to punish ORC's own
revisions. Changing a tolerance requires superseding this ADR.

## Consequences

Easier: `validation/report.md` means something; regressions in the solver
fail CI. Harder: no new free parameter may be added without a new held-out
set; tolerances are frozen here. Committed to: `calibration/` writes,
`validation/` only reads; golden corpus regenerated deliberately. Risk
accepted: the held-out gate can fail for reasons in the source polar (e.g.
the asymmetric 20 kt planing row), and the report must explain rather than
hide such cases.

**Revisit when:** a second independent data source for the J/70 (measured
boat data, another VPP) is available to validate against, or the polar is
regenerated by a current ORC VPP.

### Consequences — 2026-08-26 note (audit docs-consistency-01)

"Regressions in the solver fail CI" is not currently true: the `validate`
job is `continue-on-error: true` while the known held-out rows fail, and until
the audit's remediation `pnpm validate` always exited 0 (H-02, M-16). The gate
is reporting-only until TWS 14 `jib vmgUp` / `asym vmgDn` are closed; see
`.github/workflows/ci.yml` and `validation/report.md`. Also: the published
report scored ADR 0007's 25-row set until 2026-08-26; it now scores ADR 0012's
10 held-out rows (H-01).
